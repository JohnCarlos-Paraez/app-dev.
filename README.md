# 🎬 Favorite Movie: Interstellar

> *"Mankind was born on Earth. It was never meant to die here."*

---

## 📌 Overview
**Interstellar** is a 2014 epic science fiction film directed by *Christopher Nolan*. It follows a group of astronauts who travel through a wormhole near Saturn in search of a new home for humanity.

---

## 🍿 Key Characters & Cast
* **Joseph Cooper** – Matthew McConaughey
* **Dr. Amelia Brand** – Anne Hathaway
* **Murphy Cooper** – Jessica Chastain / Mackenzie Foy
* **TARS (Voice)** – Bill Irwin

---

## 🚀 Key Plot Phases

1. **The Blight & Discovery**  
   Earth's agriculture is collapsing due to global crop blights. Cooper discovers secret NASA coordinates.
2. **The Endurance Mission**  
   The crew launches to explore three potential habitable planets near the black hole *Gargantua*.
3. **The Tesseract & Resolution**  
   Cooper plunges into Gargantua and communicates across time to help Murphy solve the gravity equation.
